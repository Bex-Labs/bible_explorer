Our Story

Helping Kids Fall in Love with the Whole Bible

Bible Explorer started with a simple observation: most kids' devotionals circle back to the same dozen favorite stories, year after year, while huge parts of Scripture go unread. We wanted something different: a devotional that actually takes kids through the whole Bible, a few verses at a time, over the course of a few years.

🎯

Our Mission

To help every child read and understand the whole Bible by the time they finish the devotional: not just the highlight reel, but Genesis to Revelation.

🧭

How the Plan Works

Each day includes one short, age-appropriate passage and reflection. Follow it daily and you'll move steadily through the entire Bible over a few years. See our Reading Plan page for a preview.

🖨️

Print & Digital

Bible Explorer is available as a print edition worldwide via Amazon, and as free e-copies delivered by email for families who prefer reading digitally.

Who It's For

Bible Explorer is written for kids roughly ages 6–12, with language simple enough for early readers and questions substantial enough for older kids and family discussion.

Families

A shared daily rhythm parents and kids can do together, at breakfast, in the car, or before bed.

Sunday Schools & Small Groups

A ready-made structure for classes that want to cover the whole Bible over the years kids are in their program.

Homeschool Families

A simple, low-prep addition to a homeschool day that builds Bible literacy alongside reading skills.
