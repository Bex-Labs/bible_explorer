The Reading Plan

One Short Lesson a Day, the Whole Bible Over Time

Bible Explorer is organized into daily lessons that move steadily through Scripture (Old and New Testament) in an order designed to keep kids engaged and build understanding as they go. Follow along daily and you'll complete the whole Bible over a few years.

What's in Each Daily Lesson

📍

Today's Passage

A short, clearly marked Bible reference, usually just a handful of verses.

✍️

Kid-Friendly Retelling

A brief, plain-language explanation of what's happening and why it matters.

❓

Explorer Question

One reflection question to talk about together, and to post about in the community.

Sample Lessons

A few examples of what a day in Bible Explorer looks like.

Day 1 · Genesis 1:1–5

The Very Beginning

"In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth."

Explorer Question: What's one thing God made that amazes you?

Day 47 · Exodus 14:21–22

Crossing the Red Sea

"The Israelites went through the sea on dry ground."

Explorer Question: Tell about a time God helped you through something scary.

Day 214 · Matthew 5:14–16

You Are the Light of the World

"Let your light shine before others."

Explorer Question: What's one way you can "shine" for someone this week?
